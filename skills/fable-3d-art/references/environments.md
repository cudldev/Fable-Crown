# Environments

Build a plausible working place with a recognisable silhouette. The bank, forge and manor must not be the same open shed with a different sign. Use the reference's roof/wall hierarchy, substantial structural timber, inset openings, foundations and lived-in work areas while keeping decoration selective.

Resolve roof mass, wall height and opening proportions first. Show wall thickness and roof eaves. Give the roof clear large pitches and an intentional edge profile; a few coarse texture courses should carry its material. Avoid a thin floating roof sheet, mechanically identical posts everywhere or bevels on every edge. Asymmetry should follow use: a sheltered counter, chimney/firebox, stored timber, a door or service hatch.

The bank needs a readable recessed counter and banking sign, with entrance placement compatible with the game's logical doorway. The forge needs an open work face, visible dark hearth aperture, chimney, anvil and believable tool reach. Avoid decorating the space so tightly that workers have no plausible standing area. Runtime cutaways must expose actors inside, not only remove a roof while a full-height front wall hides them. Keep detachable facade/roof groups where the actual scene needs them.

Trees require a trunk-to-branch-to-canopy hierarchy. Start with an asymmetric trunk and fork; group the canopy into a dominant crown and supporting lobes. Make the outline feel leafy with a few broad scalloped breaks. Do not substitute three obvious spheres/icospheres or many independent little blobs. Cluster shading and sparse pixel leaf groups should support the crown volume rather than produce a noisy camouflage surface. Keep a single grounded trunk on the resource tile and a compatible stump state.

Review at the actual camera angle, with a character for scale. Check ground contact, door/counter height, side/back completeness, roof normals, projected silhouette and quiet material regions. Compare daytime without effects before using dusk fog or firelight to improve mood. Lighting cannot repair weak proportions.
