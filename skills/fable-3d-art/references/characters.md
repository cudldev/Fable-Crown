# Characters

For Alden, preserve brown hair, a short blue cape with restrained gold edging, cream clothing, practical boots and an adventuring silhouette. The target is a compact expressive JRPG person with RuneScape-like massing, not a doll made of rectangular blocks.

Start from the silhouette. Compare head/body ratio, shoulder width, waist, hand/boot size and the cape's negative space in front/side/back views. Shape a coherent torso and tapered limbs with deliberate cross sections. A narrowed waist, shaped collar and sleeve transitions should explain the costume without dozens of floating plates. Keep shoulders believable when the arm swings; avoid detached sleeves or an armour-like block where cloth is intended.

Build the head as a cheek/jaw/forehead form. Check the face in the normal gameplay view, not just a large front render. Tiny dark eyes, restrained brows and a small modeled nose should remain readable. Avoid oversized square eyes/eyebrows that make a LEGO face. Hair needs a dominant swept mass with a few supporting locks, a recognisable outline and controlled light/dark grouping; a spiky collection of identical wedges is not a hairstyle.

Use clothing construction: collar/lapel, belt, boot cuff/sole, cape attachment and weight. Keep blue/gold as identity accents. Paint a few broad pixel folds or shadow patches when they clarify volume. Do not create every fold as a raised strip. Reduce repetitive seams and tiny accessories that disappear at phone scale.

Current demo animation uses named rigid parts: `body`, `leftArm`, `rightArm`, `leftLeg`, `rightLeg`, `tool`, and `cape`. Shoulder/hip pivots, feet at zero and +Z GLB forward must survive export. Preview idle and walking from all sides. Check that the cape stays attached, the tool socket remains in the hand and feet contact the ground. This is an articulated draft rig, not a claim of a finished skinned character/animation library.

For a pass, identify specific image-based corrections (for example sleeve silhouette and hair grouping), implement those, export through MCP, and request lead visual review of the results. Do not add unrelated gear or change Alden's identity to create novelty.
