Largest corrections:
- Character: shape cheek/jaw and swept hair/eyes; taper cloth shoulders/waist and correct hand/tool alignment.
- Environment: strengthen purposeful roof/eave/opening hierarchy; integrate asymmetric canopy/trunk forms instead of polygon-ball lobes.
- Props: strengthen anvil horn/waist/heavy face; use coherent material values and selected wear rather than uniform thin rails.

No reference-quality candidate established by these screenshots. Crate and lantern are identifiable usable drafts at the supplied scale. Rotated, walking, roofs-off and phone comparisons remain necessary for a full visual gate.
