# Layout rules — approved interaction direction

These rules supersede earlier dashboard and shared service-tab layouts. Keep the original voxel world, pixel cast, local simulation and zero-cost/offline baseline.

1. **One presentation owns input.** Exploration, placement, an interaction, a full-screen action, or the game menu is active at a time. Context entry closes unrelated panels, hides the HUD, touch docks, tracker, FPS and notifications, and clears held input. Context exit restores exploration and live player following at the saved zoom. Held buttons must be released before acting again.
2. **Start in the world.** Only a nearby, worker-approved person or object can open its actions. The workbench crafts, a chest stores, the merchant trades, and Rowan handles the royal account. No shared service tabs or global Craft shortcut.
3. **Frame the encounter.** Smoothly move closer and lower; fit Alden and the target in the unobstructed part of the viewport. Recompute on rotation. Keep feet, billboard scale and world grounding. Reduced motion uses immediate framing. Preserve the exact exploration camera settings.
4. **Conversations have turns.** Show a short NPC line, then natural player replies or actions. Dialogue and replies replace one another. No visible Leave/Back navigation in NPC chats. A terminal line ends when advanced; never dismiss unread speech on a timer. Hardware cancel remains available.
5. **Actions may fill the screen.** Choosing “Craft something” at a bench opens a focused full-screen crafting layout. Back returns to that bench prompt. Other action screens retain the same contextual origin.
6. **Browse and inspect together.** Crafting keeps illustrated recipes, selected item, actual carried/required materials and one craft action visible together. Selecting or focusing a recipe updates details in place. Keep the selection after crafting.
7. **Reserve roughly one third for information.** In landscape, including 16:9, the right information column occupies about one third of the screen width. In portrait the bottom information panel occupies about one third of its height. Include materials, feedback, batch controls and the craft action inside that allocation. The recipe region scrolls independently; keep the primary action reachable. Small screens may grow the information area to preserve readable text and 44px targets.
8. **Use pictures and quiet surfaces.** Original runtime item imagery, flat parchment, dark ink and restrained gold selection. Sparse labels; no decorative statistics or repeated descriptions. Never copy another game's assets or branding.
9. **Make outcomes clear.** Show missing requirements before confirmation, pending/progress, exact rewards and output destination. Keep related feedback within the active interaction; defer unrelated notifications. Prevent repeated transactions while a request is pending.
10. **Support all inputs.** At least 44px targets; native keyboard focus and a trapped dialog; controller directions select/focus, A confirms and B returns or dismisses. Touch needs no hover or typing. Cancel touches on menu entry and orientation change. Preserve interaction selection on rotation.
11. **Show controls for the active device.** Desktop starts with keyboard/mouse and no virtual joystick or touch action buttons. Mobile shows touch controls until intentional controller use; an idle connected pad or stick drift does not switch modes. Touch brings them back, and disconnect restores the appropriate layout. Switching releases held input; movement/menu navigation continue through the chosen device. Keep device state out of game saves.
12. **Follow and face.** Exploration always follows Alden, gently leading actual movement and settling when stopped. Dragging and the right stick cannot park the camera; wheel/pinch adjusts zoom. Face a nearby target to reveal its automatic action marker. The small ring and facing arrow at Alden's feet are depth-tested geometry in the 3D world, conforming to terrain at fixed world scale. Touch world taps never select actions. Mouse activation requires actual interaction range, turns toward the chosen target, and consumes the click. Placement retains explicit touch selection. Hide both markers during other presentations; keep the legacy chapter camera independent.

Acceptance: actual WebGL captures at 320×568, 390×844, 667×375, 844×390 and desktop 16:9; no covered primary actions, horizontal overflow, simultaneous HUD/interaction, duplicate rewards or lost current saves. Run GPU tests locally, keep release CI lightweight, and verify the public PWA after deployment. Mockups alone do not establish implementation acceptance.
